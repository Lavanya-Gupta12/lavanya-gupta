Eight stations, a few repairs

For the network coordinator · Read before the spring figures are shared

Where should the technician go?

Send the technician to S04 — Foothills Community Garden.

The daytime temperature has been reading too warm, with the difference growing since late March. The pattern points to a radiation shield that may have become blocked after the 15 March repaint. Its rain gauge also appears to have been blocked by leaves.

  • Check the shield slats first and clear any obstruction.
  • Compare the station reading with a hand thermometer held in the shade.
  • Fit a leaf guard, then test the rain gauge with a measured cup of water.

Runner-up: S03. Its temperature sensor is failing, but the errors are obvious and have been marked for automatic removal. S04 is the better use of the one-hour visit because both temperature and rainfall need attention.

Looks wrong, but is real

S01 is much colder than the others because it sits high up (2,913 metres). Once altitude is allowed for, its temperature is in line with the network. Keep it.

S01’s rain on otherwise dry days is likely snowmelt. Those days had below-freezing minimum temperatures, so keep the rain-gauge readings rather than treating them as a fault.
